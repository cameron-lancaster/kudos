Kudos Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Kudos keeps a friendly tally of favors between friends. We collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and there are no ads or tracking.

Who we are

Kudos is made by Cameron Lancaster. Questions about this policy or your data: cameronlancaster@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Apple sign-in. You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an account identifier, and on your first sign-in only, your first name if you choose to share it. We don't ask Apple for your email address.
  • Your profile. The display name you choose and, if you add one, your profile photo.
  • What you do in groups. Groups you create or join, Kudos you send and receive, requests, notes, tags, comments, reactions, and likes.

We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or device identifiers for advertising, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

Only to run the app: showing your groups, balances, and activity to you and the friends in your groups. Your profile name and photo are visible to people you share a group with. A request you send or receive is visible only to the two of you.

Who can see it

  • Friends in your groups see your name, photo, and the group's activity.
  • People outside your groups can't see anything, except that someone with a group's invite link sees the group's name, emoji, and a few members' names and photos before joining.
  • Service providers. Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and photos. Sign-in is handled by Apple.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Keeping it and deleting it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account anytime in the app under Profile → Delete account. When you do, we:

  • delete your profile photo, comments, reactions, and likes;
  • remove you from every group and delete your sign-in;
  • replace your name with "Former member" on Kudos you sent or received, so your friends' balances still add up.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Our database only lets you read what you're allowed to see and only lets you change your own things; every balance change is checked on our server.

Children

Kudos isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
